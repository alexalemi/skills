## Part III: Land Development Code (PTIIILADECO) ⭐ Most PAB-Relevant

**NodeId Prefix:** `PTIIILADECO`
**Total Entries:** 298 rows

This section is the most important for Planning Advisory Board work.

### ⭐ Critical PAB Authority Sections in Part III

**§14-3-1 - Planning Advisory Board** ⭐⭐⭐
- PAB designated as local planning agency (F.S. §163.3174)
- Review and recommend on comprehensive plan amendments
- Review land development regulations for consistency
- Review zoning map amendments
- Review conditional uses

**§14-3-16 - Level of Review Required** ⭐⭐
- Table showing which applications require PAB review
- Critical reference for determining PAB jurisdiction

**§14-3-20 - Public Hearings** ⭐⭐
- PAB can: Approve, Approve with conditions, Continue, or Deny
- Authority to impose conditions on approvals

**§14-3-21 - Appeals** ⭐
- PAB decisions may be appealed to city commission within 10 days
- Commission bases decision on code review criteria

**§14-3-23 - Comprehensive Plan Text and Map Amendments** ⭐⭐⭐
- Lists 7 criteria for future land use amendments
- Consistency requirements with comp plan

**§14-3-29 - Conditional Uses** ⭐⭐⭐ (FINAL APPROVAL AUTHORITY)
- **PAB has final approval authority** (not just recommendation)
- Decisions final after 10 days unless appealed
- Exception: Applications by PAB member, commissioner, or city employee go to commission

**§14-3-35 - Designation of Historic Districts** ⭐
- PAB involvement in historic district designation

**§14-3-36 - Designation of Historic Landmarks** ⭐
- PAB involvement in landmark designation

**§14-3-47 - Vacation and Abandonment of Rights-of-Way and Easements** ⭐
- PAB review of ROW vacation applications

**§14-4-6 - Regulations for Zoning Districts** ⭐
- Footnote 1: Minimum 5-acre tract for Business Park may be waived by commission upon PAB recommendation

**§14-4-8 - Planned Unit Developments** ⭐⭐
- Section (4)(b)ii: **Building separation requirements may be partially waived** by commission after PAB review
- Must consider impact on light, air, privacy, fire hazards

**§14-6-8 - Special Events** ⭐
- DRC decisions may be appealed to commission following PAB recommendation

**§14-6-40 - Drive-Through Facility** ⭐
- PAB can consider alternate locations for drive-through windows/menu boards on case-by-case basis

**§14-7-22 - Automobile Parking Ratios** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Section (E)2: Parking may be reduced by up to 50%** for non-residential in Traditional Neighborhood Developments
- Commission makes final decision after PAB review
- PAB determines if uses primarily serve neighborhood residents/businesses

**§14-11-8 - Off-Site Signs** ⭐
- PAB review of off-site sign applications

**§14-11-16 - True Works of Art** ⭐⭐ (APPROVAL AUTHORITY)
- **PAB must approve** all murals, statuary, and similar outdoor items
- Must determine: (1) True art, not sign, and (2) Compatible with surrounding area
- Director can approve if customary yard/building accessory

## Usage Tips

### For Comprehensive Plan Amendments (CPAs)
Focus on:
- Chapter 14-1 (Introduction and Applicability)
- Chapter 14-4 (Zoning) - to understand implications of land use changes

### For Rezoning Applications
Focus on:
- Chapter 14-3 (Development Review Procedures)
- Chapter 14-4 (Zoning) - review old and new district regulations
- Chapter 14-7 (Parking) - verify compliance with parking requirements

### For Site Plans and Plats
Focus on:
- Chapter 14-10 (Subdivision and Site Design)
- Chapter 14-7 (Access and Parking)
- Chapter 14-8 (Landscaping)
- Chapter 14-9 (Environment)

### For Variances and Special Exceptions
Focus on:
- Chapter 14-3 (Development Review Procedures)
- Chapter 14-4 (Zoning) - understand what standards are being varied
- Chapter 14-6 (Special Use Standards)
