## 2-210 Composition and terms

> (a) Membership. The _planning_ _advisory_ _board_ is hereby established and shall consist of eight members.
>
> 	(1) Seven voting members, at least five members shall be city residents and the two remaining members may be persons who either own real property or maintain a business office within the city, and are appointed by the city commission; and
>
> 	(2) One nonvoting member, appointed by the school _board_, representing the school district of the county. The member of the _board_ appointed by the school district may only comment on comprehensive _plan_ amendments and zoning changes that would increase residential density on the subject property. This nonvoting member is not required to be a resident of the city. The city commission may recommend an individual to the school _board_ for consideration to this appointment.
>
> (b) Terms. Terms of the seven voting members of the _board_ shall begin on June 1 and run through May 31 for three years. The school _board_ appointed member shall serve at the pleasure of the school district of the county.
>
> (c) Vacancies. Any vacancy in the voting membership shall be filled for the unexpired term by the commission who shall have the authority to remove any member for cause, upon written charges, after a public hearing. Any vacancy in the school _board_ appointed position shall be filled by the school _board_ which shall have the authority to remove an appointee its discretion.
>
> (d) Attendance. A voting member of the _planning_ _advisory_ _board_ missing three consecutive regular meetings, without justification acceptable to the _planning_ _advisory_ _board_, shall be considered resigned; in no case shall a member miss more than six regular meetings per calendar year. The nonvoting member's attendance shall be reported to the county school district on a periodic basis.
>
> (e) Compensation. Members of the _planning_ _advisory_ _board_ shall receive no salaries or fees for service on the _planning_ _advisory_ _board_ but may receive actual and necessary expenses incurred in the performance of their duties of office. The school district shall be responsible for and shall pay to the city all expenses incurred by the school district's appointee.

## 14-3-20 - Public hearings

>

>
> i. A change in conditions affecting the parcel.
> ii. A deficiency in the amount of land available for uses allowed by the designation.
> iii. The special suitability of the property for the proposed designation.
> iv. An increase in land use compatibility which would result from the change.
